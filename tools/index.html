Why these exist

I kept needing these numbers while building software for small businesses, and the sites I found wanted an email address before they would tell me what my EMI was. So I built my own and left them open.

Three rules apply to every tool here. The calculation happens in your browser, so whatever you type never reaches a server. There is no account and no limit on how often you use them. And each tool explains the formula behind it, because a number you cannot check is not much use.

If a tool gives you a figure that looks wrong, tell me. Corrections get made the same week. My email is hello@khanjankavani.com.

One thing to be clear about. These are general purpose calculators, not financial or tax advice. Rates and rules in India change, sometimes mid year. Check anything important against the current rules or a qualified professional before you act on it. The full note is on the disclaimer page.
